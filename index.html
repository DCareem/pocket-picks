How this is counted

  • Picks are detected in KR ranked solo games, patch 16.19, sampled from the Platinum-to-Challenger ladders and the lobby-mates of Emerald+ players. Each of the 10 players counts in their own ladder tier, so only Platinum+ players' champions are included (138,195 champion appearances). Remakes and every queue other than ranked solo/duo are excluded. EUW1, ME1, RU, TR1, EUN1, OC1, BR1, NA1, LA1, LA2, JP1, TW2, SG2, VN2 (55,671 games) are used only to show whether a pick has spread.
  • One-tricks: a combo normally needs at least 8 different players, with no single player above 35% of its games. A combo carried by one player is still shown, marked "one player", when that player alone has at least 30 games on it and clears the same win-rate bar (7 one-player combos did not). No player names are shown.
  • PBE: champions and items whose numbers change between live 16.19 and PBE 16.20 are flagged — a pick can die next patch.
  • Community: posts in r/leagueoflegends, r/summonerschool and Inven's LoL boards (Korea, titles only) since the patch went live (2026-09-23 18:09 UTC) that a language model judged to be recommending that champion in that role. Lots of talk means it is no longer early.
  • Off-role: the champion plays this role in under 20% of its games, its pick rate in the role is under 0.5%, it has at least 30 games, and its win rate's lower bound is above 50%.
  • Unusual item: built in under 15% of that champion's games in that role, at least 30 games, a win rate at least 7% points above the champion's other builds, and a lower bound above both 50% and those builds.
  • Unusual keystone or summoner spells: taken by under 15% of that champion's players in that role, at least 30 games from several different players, a win rate at least 7% points above the champion's other choices, and a lower bound above both 50% and those choices. Counted on the 52,765 champion appearances that carry rune data (games stored from 26 September).
  • Items are read from end-of-game inventories. Winners play longer games and buy "luxury" items once they are ahead, so only games that ended with 2 or 3 finished items count (the item was a core buy), each compared with games that finished exactly as many. Upgraded forms (Manamune and Muramana, ...) count as one item.
  • Small samples lie. A pick here is a lead worth trying, not a guarantee.
