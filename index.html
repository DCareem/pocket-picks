How this is counted

  • Picks are detected in KR ranked solo games, patch 16.19, sampled from the Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Challenger ladders and their Diamond+ lobby-mates. Remakes are excluded. EUW1 (6,897 games) are used only to show whether a pick has spread.
  • No one-tricks: a combo needs at least 8 different players, and no single player may account for more than 35% of its games (0 combos dropped this patch).
  • PBE: champions and items whose numbers change between live 16.19 and PBE 16.20 are flagged — a pick can die next patch.
  • Community: posts in r/leagueoflegends, r/summonerschool and Inven's LoL boards (Korea, titles only) since the patch went live (not scanned yet) that a language model judged to be recommending that champion in that role. Lots of talk means it is no longer early.
  • Off-role: the champion plays this role in under 20% of its games, its pick rate in the role is under 0.5%, it has at least 30 games, and its win rate's lower bound is above 50%.
  • Unusual item: built in under 15% of that champion's games in that role, at least 30 games, lower bound above both 50% and the champion's other builds.
  • Items are read from end-of-game inventories. Winners play longer games and buy "luxury" items once they are ahead, so only games that ended with 2 or 3 finished items count (the item was a core buy), each compared with games that finished exactly as many. Upgraded forms (Manamune and Muramana, ...) count as one item.
  • Small samples lie. A pick here is a lead worth trying, not a guarantee.
