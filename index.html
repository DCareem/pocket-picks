How this is counted

  • Sources: Riot's official API (ranked ladders and match data), CommunityDragon (live and PBE game files), Data Dragon (names and icons), Meraki Analytics (each champion's established positions), Reddit and Inven (public posts since patch day). Chinese servers are not in Riot's API and are not collected.
  • Picks are detected in KR ranked solo games, patch 16.19, sampled from the Platinum-to-Challenger ladders and the lobby-mates of Emerald+ players. Each of the 10 players counts in their own ladder tier, so only Platinum+ players' champions are included (566,399 champion appearances). Remakes and every queue other than ranked solo/duo are excluded. EUW1, NA1, BR1, OC1, VN2 (106,052 games) are used only to show whether a pick has spread.
  • By rank: every pick also shows its win rate among Platinum+, Emerald+ and Diamond+ players (each player at their own rank), with the number of games. Green or red marks a higher band at least 3 points above or below Platinum+, so you can see whether stronger players win more or less with it.
  • One-tricks: a combo normally needs at least 8 different players, with no single player above 35% of its games. A combo carried by one player is still shown, marked "one player", when that player alone has at least 30 games on it and clears the same win-rate bar (0 one-player combos did not). No player names are shown.
  • PBE: champions and items whose numbers change between live 16.19 and PBE 16.20 are flagged — a pick can die next patch.
  • Community: posts in r/leagueoflegends, r/summonerschool and Inven's LoL boards (Korea, titles only) since the patch went live (not scanned yet) that a language model judged to be recommending that champion in that role. Lots of talk means it is no longer early.
  • Off-role: the role is not one of the champion's established positions (community list by Meraki Analytics; for champions not on it, under 10% of their games worldwide), its pick rate in Korea is under 0.5%, and it has at least 30 games. Listed when the cautious win rate is above 50%; Watch when the win rate is at least 5% points above the bracket average.
  • Unusual item: built by under 8% of that champion's players in that role worldwide and not one of its 6 most-built items, at least 30 games, a win rate at least 7% points above the champion's other builds, and a lower bound above both 50% and those builds.
  • Unusual keystone: taken by under 5% of that champion's players in that role worldwide and not one of its 3 most common keystones, at least 30 games from several different players, a win rate at least 7% points above the champion's other keystones, and a lower bound above both 50% and those keystones. Counted on the 478,503 champion appearances that carry rune data (games stored from 26 September).
  • Items are read from end-of-game inventories. Winners play longer games and buy "luxury" items once they are ahead, so only games that ended with 2 or 3 finished items count (the item was a core buy), each compared with games that finished exactly as many. Upgraded forms (Manamune and Muramana, ...) count as one item.
  • Small samples lie. A pick here is a lead worth trying, not a guarantee.
